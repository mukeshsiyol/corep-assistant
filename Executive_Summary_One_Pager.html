🎯 The Problem

UK banks spend 40+ hours per quarter manually interpreting dense PRA Rulebook and EBA guidance to prepare COREP returns. This labor-intensive process is error-prone, inconsistent across analysts, and lacks audit traceability—creating regulatory risk.

💡 Proposed Solution

What It Does

  • Answers analyst questions: "Where do I report retained earnings?"
  • Maps business scenarios to COREP template fields
  • Provides regulatory citations (PRA Rulebook, CRR articles)
  • Validates outputs against COREP rules
  • Generates audit trails for examiner queries

How It's Different

  • Schema-constrained outputs (prevents hallucination)
  • Human-in-the-loop validation required
  • Every field traceable to source regulation
  • Scoped to 2 templates (C 01.00, C 02.00)
  • Decision-support tool, not automation

🛡️ Risk Mitigation

Model Risk Controls

  • Human approval required for all outputs
  • Confidence scoring (medium/low → review)
  • Quarterly LLM audits vs. manual submissions
  • Dual control for high-materiality fields

Data Security

  • On-premises deployment (no external APIs)
  • Role-based access controls
  • 7-year audit log retention (PRA compliant)
  • Escalation process for ambiguous cases
